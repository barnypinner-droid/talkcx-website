TalkCX has partnered with Avant. Why that means there's always a solution

← All posts

23 November 2026 · Barny Pinner

I'm pleased to say TalkCX is now a Trusted Advisor with Avant Communications, a leading technology services distributor.

The short version: Avant works with a very large number of technology partners. That means whatever problem you bring to us, there's always a solution we can put in front of you.

One problem, lots of possible answers

Every business I speak to has a slightly different problem. A dental group missing calls at lunchtime. A contact centre stuck on an ageing phone system. A team that wants AI to take routine calls but needs it to work with the systems they already have.

No single vendor is the right answer for all of those. When an adviser only has one or two products to offer, the temptation is to make every problem fit them. That's not how I want to work.

What Avant brings

Avant has built partnerships with suppliers across the whole technology stack, including:

  • Customer experience and contact centre platforms
  • Conversational and voice AI
  • Phone systems and unified communications
  • Connectivity, cloud and security

Because Avant already works with so many partners, I can compare options side by side and find the one that genuinely fits your business, your budget and the systems you already run. And if your problem crosses several of those areas, as most do, I can bring the right pieces together.

What it means for you

  • There's always a solution. With so many partners to draw on, you're not limited to what one vendor happens to make.
  • Advice based on fit. I recommend what suits you, not what I happen to sell.
  • Specialist backup. Avant gives me access to supplier specialists and engineering support for the more technical projects.
  • One point of contact. You deal with me from the first conversation through to go-live, however many suppliers are involved.

What doesn't change

Every engagement still starts with understanding your calls, your customers and your team. I'll tell you honestly what will help and what won't. Having so many options just means the answer is far more likely to be the right one.

If you're reviewing your contact centre or phone setup, looking at AI, or have a contract renewal coming up, I'd be happy to talk it through.
