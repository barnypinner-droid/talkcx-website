TalkCX is now an Avant Trusted Advisor. Here's what that means for you

← All posts

23 November 2026 · Barny Pinner

I'm pleased to say TalkCX is now a Trusted Advisor with Avant Communications, one of the largest technology services distributors in the channel.

That's a bit of industry jargon, so here's what it actually means, and why it matters if you're choosing contact centre, voice AI or communications technology.

What Avant does

Avant works with a large portfolio of technology suppliers: contact centre platforms, conversational AI, phone systems, connectivity, security and more. Independent advisors like TalkCX work through Avant to help businesses research, compare and buy from those suppliers.

Avant also gives its advisors access to research tools, supplier specialists and engineering support. In plain terms, I get a much bigger bench behind me.

Why I've done it

TalkCX was set up to be vendor-neutral. I've spent 25 years in contact centres, and plenty of that on the vendor side, so I know how easy it is for "advice" to lead to whichever product the adviser happens to sell.

I'm an official PolyAI reseller, and I think PolyAI is excellent at what it does. But it isn't the answer to every problem. Sometimes the real issue is the contact centre platform, the phone system or the connectivity underneath it. Working with Avant means I can recommend what genuinely fits, from a much wider choice of suppliers, rather than bending the problem to fit one product.

What it means for you

  • More choice, one conversation. Voice AI, CCaaS, telephony and the pieces in between, compared side by side.
  • Independent advice. My job is to find the right fit for you, not to push a particular vendor.
  • Specialist backup. Where a project needs deeper technical input, there's an engineering team behind me.
  • One point of contact. You deal with me from the first call through to go-live, whichever suppliers end up involved.

What doesn't change

The way I work stays the same. Every engagement starts with understanding your calls, your customers and your team. I'll tell you honestly where technology will help, and where it won't. And if the answer is "you don't need anything new", I'll say so.

If you're reviewing your contact centre setup, looking at voice AI, or your contract renewal is coming up, I'd be happy to talk it through.

A 30-minute call, no obligation. We'll tell you where AI would genuinely help, and where it wouldn't.
