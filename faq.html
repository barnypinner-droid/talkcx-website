FAQ

Contact Centre AI: your questions answered.

Straight answers on what AI call centre agents cost, how they work with your team, and how TalkCX sets them up.

About TalkCX

What does TalkCX do?

TalkCX is a vendor-neutral Contact Centre AI consultancy and an official PolyAI reseller partner. We help UK contact centres, call centres and local businesses choose, set up and run AI voice agents, AI receptionists and digital agents, from readiness assessment and vendor selection to go-live and support.

Are you independent if you resell PolyAI?

We tell you up front that we're a PolyAI partner. In a vendor selection we score every vendor against the same criteria you've agreed, and you make the decision.

Which vendors do you cover?

The main CCaaS and conversational AI platforms sold in the UK. We agree the long list with you.

Is there a minimum size?

No. Local packages start at one site.

How Contact Centre AI works

Which AI does TalkCX use?

Our voice and digital agents run on PolyAI, and TalkCX is an official PolyAI reseller partner. We design, set up and support the agent for you.

Will callers know it's an AI?

Yes. The agent says it's an AI assistant at the start of the call, and callers can ask for a person.

How do you decide what the AI should hand over to a person?

We agree it with you. Complaints, vulnerable callers and anything the AI can't complete safely usually go straight to a person.

What is a digital conversation?

One conversation with a customer on your website chat, WhatsApp or SMS, however many messages it takes.

What if AI isn't right for us?

Then our assessment says so. Sometimes the better answer is a process or IVR change, and we'll tell you that.

Integrations

What systems can the AI agent connect to?

Our agents run on PolyAI, which connects to phone systems, CRMs such as Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Zendesk, booking systems such as OpenTable, healthcare systems such as Epic, knowledge tools, and any other system with an API. We confirm exactly what your agent will connect to during scoping.

Will it work with our phone system?

Almost certainly. PolyAI works with Genesys Cloud, NICE CXone, Five9, Amazon Connect, Twilio, Zoom, Dialpad and Zendesk Talk, and with any phone system through a standard SIP connection. For smaller sites, you can simply forward calls from your existing number.

Can it transfer calls to my team?

Yes. When a call needs a person, the agent transfers it through your phone system and can pass the caller's details along with it, so your team doesn't start from scratch.

Can it look up and update customer records?

Yes. Connected to your CRM or booking system, the agent can check an account, find or change a booking and log what happened on the call, using the same rules your team follows.

Does it work on webchat, SMS and WhatsApp?

Yes. The same agent can answer on webchat, SMS and WhatsApp, and hand a chat to your team in Salesforce, Zendesk, NICE CXone, Genesys Cloud, Amazon Connect, Five9 or Webex Contact Center.

What if our system isn't on the list?

If it has an API, we can usually connect it. We check this early in the project, before you commit to a build.

Are the connections secure?

Yes. Calls are encrypted by default, system passwords and keys are stored securely in the platform rather than in the agent itself, and every request the agent makes to your systems is logged so it can be reviewed.

Who sets up the integrations?

We do. We work with your IT team or phone supplier, so we just need someone who knows your systems and access to set up the connection.

Pricing and contracts

What is a token?

Tokens are how plans are measured. One token covers 30 seconds of an AI voice call or one digital conversation, so a voice minute uses 2 tokens. Your tokens can go on calls, chat or any mix.

How much does Contact Centre AI cost?

Local business plans start at £100 a month per site, and plans for growing businesses and contact centres start at £625 a month on a 3-year term. Pay as you go is 10p a token. See the pricing page for every plan. Prices exclude VAT.

What happens if I go over my allowance?

Nothing stops working. Extra usage is charged at 10p a token (20p a voice minute, 10p a digital conversation), billed monthly. If you go over regularly, we'll suggest the next package up.

Is there a contract?

Local plans run for 24 months per site with free setup, like a phone contract. A 12-month contract is available with a £295 setup fee. Plans for growing businesses run for 3 years.

Can I change package?

Yes. You can move up a package at any time. Moving down is possible at the end of each 12 months of your contract.

Do prices include VAT?

No. All prices exclude VAT.

Projects and support

Do we need to have chosen a vendor first?

No. Our AI readiness assessment is vendor-neutral and often happens before you've spoken to anyone.

What do you need from us to get started?

Access to call reports or recordings, an hour or two with a few team members, and someone who knows your systems.

Who supports the agent after launch?

We do. Ongoing support and growth packages are available if you want us to keep improving it.

Can you join a project that's already late?

Yes. We'll start with a quick review of where things stand.
