Questions

Conversational AI Design: common questions

Is design included in the plans?

Designing the agent we set up for you is part of setup on our plans. Design for extra use cases, or for another platform, is priced separately.

Do you only design for PolyAI?

No. We can design for any platform and hand the design to your vendor.

How do you decide what the AI should hand over?

We agree it with you. Complaints, vulnerable callers and anything the AI can't complete safely usually go straight to a person.
