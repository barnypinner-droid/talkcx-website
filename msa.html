2. How this agreement works

2.1 Order Forms. Each Order Form, together with this MSA and any SOW and Vendor Terms it refers to, forms a separate contract. You may buy further Services at any time by signing a new Order Form.

2.2 Order of precedence. If there is a conflict, the following order applies: (a) the DPA, for anything relating to Personal Data; (b) the Order Form; (c) the SOW; (d) this MSA; and (e) the Documentation. Vendor Terms apply to your use of the Platform and prevail over this MSA only to the extent they place additional restrictions on that use.

2.3 No other terms. Terms in your purchase orders, supplier onboarding portals or similar documents do not apply, even if we accept or acknowledge them, unless both parties expressly agree in writing that they amend this Agreement.

2.4 Affiliates. Your affiliates may buy Services under this MSA by signing their own Order Form, in which case they are the Customer for that Order Form.

3. Platform Services

3.1 Right to use. Subject to payment of the Fees and the terms of this Agreement, we grant you a non-exclusive, non-transferable right, without the right to sublicense, to access and use the Platform Services during the term of the relevant Order Form for your internal business purposes, including making AI agents available to your End Users.

3.2 Reseller arrangement. The Platform is owned and operated by the Platform Provider, and we supply it to you as an authorised reseller and implementation partner. The Platform Provider may update, improve or change Platform functionality from time to time. Where a change materially reduces the functionality you have bought, we will tell you as soon as reasonably practicable after we are notified and work with you on alternatives.

3.3 Vendor Terms. You will comply with any Vendor Terms named in an Order Form. We will give you a copy of them before you sign.

3.4 Service levels and support. We provide support in line with Schedule 1. Platform availability targets and any service credits are those stated in the Order Form, which reflect the commitments we receive from the Platform Provider. Service credits are your sole financial remedy for the Platform failing to meet an availability target.

3.5 Maintenance. Planned maintenance will be notified in advance where we receive notice from the Platform Provider, and scheduled where possible outside your core operating hours. Emergency maintenance may happen without notice where needed to protect the security or stability of the Platform.

3.6 Early-access features. We may offer features labelled as beta, preview or early access. They are optional, are provided without any service level or warranty, may change or be withdrawn at any time, and should not be used for critical processes.

3.7 Suspension. We may suspend all or part of the Platform Services, giving as much notice as is reasonable in the circumstances, if: (a) it is necessary to comply with law or the instruction of a regulator; (b) there is a security threat to the Platform, Customer Data or other customers; (c) the Platform Provider suspends our access in respect of your use; (d) you breach clause 5.8; or (e) any undisputed Fees remain unpaid 14 days after we give you written notice of non-payment. We will restore the Services promptly once the reason for suspension is resolved.

4. Project Services and Consultancy Services

4.1 SOWs. Each SOW will describe the scope, Deliverables, assumptions, dependencies, milestones and Fees (in money or Tokens) for the work it covers.

4.2 Standard of work. We will perform Project Services and Consultancy Services with reasonable skill and care, using suitably experienced people, and in line with good industry practice.

4.3 Dependencies. Our delivery depends on you meeting the responsibilities in clause 5 and the dependencies in the SOW. If you do not, we will tell you, the timetable will be extended to reflect the delay, and we may charge for any additional effort at the rates in the Order Form.

4.4 Testing and acceptance. When we tell you an AI agent or other Deliverable is ready for user acceptance testing, you will have 10 Business Days to test it and tell us in writing of any material failure to meet the SOW. We will fix reported failures and re-submit. A Deliverable is accepted when you confirm acceptance, when the testing period ends without a notice of material failure, or at Go-Live, whichever happens first.

4.5 Changes. Either party may raise a Change Request. We will tell you the effect on scope, timetable and Fees (including any Token cost). No change is binding until both parties agree it in writing, and email agreement is enough.

4.6 Advice and outcomes. Our Consultancy Services are vendor-neutral and based on the information available to us at the time. You remain responsible for your own business decisions. Any projected outcomes, such as cost savings, call containment rates or customer satisfaction scores, are estimates and not guarantees.

4.7 Subcontractors. We may use subcontractors and the Platform Provider to deliver Services. We remain responsible for their performance under this Agreement.

5. Your responsibilities

5.1 Compliance with law. You will use the Services in compliance with all laws and regulatory requirements that apply to you, including data protection, call recording, telecommunications and direct marketing rules (including the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 and Ofcom requirements) and any rules of your sector regulator or professional body.

5.2 Transparency to End Users. You are responsible for telling End Users that they are interacting with an automated AI agent where the law requires it or End Users would reasonably expect to be told, for informing them about call recording, and for providing any privacy information and obtaining any consents the law requires.

5.3 Human escalation and sensitive use. You will maintain an appropriate route for End Users to reach a human, especially for urgent, complex or sensitive matters and for vulnerable people. AI agents must not be used to give medical, clinical, legal or financial advice beyond the scripted information agreed in the SOW, or as a substitute for emergency services. Healthcare and dental customers remain responsible for clinical triage and for meeting the standards of their regulators.

5.4 Your content. You will provide accurate, complete and up-to-date information for AI agents to use (for example opening hours, prices, policies and FAQs), and tell us promptly when it changes.

5.5 Access to your systems. Where Services require integration with your telephony, booking, practice management, CRM or other systems, you will provide timely access, credentials and technical contacts, and you confirm you have the rights and third-party approvals needed for us and the Platform Provider to access them.

5.6 Accounts and security. You are responsible for keeping user accounts and credentials secure, for the actions of your users, and for telling us promptly of any suspected unauthorised access.

5.7 Project lead. You will appoint a named person with authority to make decisions and to attend agreed project meetings.

5.8 Restrictions. You will not, and will not allow anyone else to: (a) copy, modify, decompile or reverse engineer the Platform except as permitted by law; (b) resell, sublicense or provide the Services to third parties except your End Users; (c) use the Services to build a competing product or to train a competing AI model; (d) use the Services for unsolicited or nuisance calls or messages, fraud, harassment, impersonation of real people without their consent, or any unlawful, harmful or deceptive purpose; (e) use the Services to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on individuals without meaningful human review; (f) introduce malicious code or try to circumvent security, usage limits or safety controls; or (g) publish benchmarks of the Platform without our prior written consent. A breach of this clause is a material breach.

6. Fees, Tokens and payment

6.1 Fees. You will pay the Fees in the Order Form or SOW. Fees are in pounds sterling and exclude VAT and other taxes, which you will pay at the applicable rate.

6.2 Invoicing. Unless the Order Form says otherwise, we will invoice Platform Services and Token packages annually in advance, Project Services against the milestones in the SOW, and Consultancy Services monthly in arrears.

6.3 Payment. You will pay each invoice within 30 days of the invoice date, to the account shown on the invoice.

6.4 Disputed invoices. If you dispute an invoice in good faith, you will tell us in writing within 14 days of receiving it, with reasons, and pay the undisputed part on time. Both parties will work to resolve the dispute promptly.

6.5 Late payment. We may charge interest on overdue undisputed amounts under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998, and may suspend Services under clause 3.7.

6.6 Tokens. Tokens are drawn down at the rates in the Order Form. Unless the Order Form says otherwise: (a) unused Tokens expire at the end of the Contract Year in which they were bought and do not roll over; (b) Tokens have no cash value and are not refundable; and (c) usage above your Token balance is invoiced monthly in arrears at the overage rate in the Order Form. We will provide a statement of Token usage on request and at least monthly.

6.7 Pass-through costs. Telephony minutes, phone numbers, SMS, third-party integration licences and similar costs charged to us by third parties for your use are payable by you as set out in the Order Form.

6.8 Expenses. We will only charge travel and other expenses that you have approved in advance.

6.9 Price changes. Fees are fixed for the initial term of each Order Form. We may change Fees for any renewal term by giving you at least 60 days' written notice before the renewal date, including to reflect price changes by the Platform Provider.

6.10 Commitment. Fees for committed Services under an Order Form are non-cancellable and non-refundable, except where this MSA expressly provides for a refund.

7.1 Term of this MSA. This MSA starts when the first Order Form is signed and continues while any Order Form remains in force.

7.2 Term of Order Forms. Each Order Form runs for the initial term it states. Unless it says otherwise, it then renews automatically for successive periods of 12 months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the current term.

7.3 Termination for cause. Either party may terminate an Order Form, or this MSA and all Order Forms, by written notice if the other party: (a) commits a material breach which is not capable of remedy, or which is not remedied within 30 days of written notice describing it; or (b) becomes insolvent, enters administration, liquidation or any arrangement with its creditors, or anything similar happens in any jurisdiction.

7.4 Platform withdrawal. If the Platform Provider stops making the Platform available to us for reasons outside our control, we will tell you promptly and propose an alternative. If we cannot agree an alternative within 30 days, either party may terminate the affected Order Form by written notice, and we will refund any prepaid Fees for Platform Services not yet provided and any unexpired Tokens bought for that Platform.

7.5 Effect of termination. When an Order Form ends: (a) your right to use the relevant Services ends; (b) you will pay all Fees for Services provided up to the end date; (c) if we terminate under clause 7.3 for your breach, all remaining Fees for the committed term become due; (d) if you terminate under clause 7.3 for our breach, we will refund any prepaid Fees for the period after termination; (e) Customer Data will be returned or deleted in line with the DPA; and (f) each party will return or destroy the other's Confidential Information on request, except where retention is required by law.

7.6 Survival. Clauses which by their nature are intended to survive, including clauses 6, 7.5, 8, 9, 12, 13 and 14, continue after termination.
