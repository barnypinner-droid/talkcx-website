In-House AI receptionist

An AI receptionist that works alongside your team, all day. When the phones are busy it picks up the calls your team can't get to, answers common questions and passes urgent calls straight through. When you're closed, it covers everything.

What it does

  • Everything in Out of Hours
  • Answers overflow calls during opening hours when your team is busy
  • Answers common questions: opening times, prices, directions, what to bring
  • Routes urgent calls to the right person
  • Transfers callers to your team when someone is free
  • Handles website chat, WhatsApp or SMS at any time

It takes booking requests but doesn't put them in your diary. To book, move and cancel appointments automatically, see Connected.

Questions

In-House: common questions

Is there a contract?

Yes, 24 months per site with free setup, like a phone contract. A 12-month contract is available with a £295 setup fee.

What if I use more than my allowance?

It keeps working. Extra use is 10p a token (20p per voice minute, 10p per digital conversation), billed monthly. If it happens often, we'll suggest a bigger package.

Can I change package later?

Yes. You can move up a package at any time.

Will callers know it's an AI?

Yes. The agent says it's an AI assistant at the start of the call, and callers can ask for a person.

Which AI is it?

It runs on PolyAI. TalkCX is an official PolyAI reseller partner and sets it up and supports it for you.

Do prices include VAT?

No. All prices exclude VAT.
