Treatments

Everything your smile needs, under one roof

From a child's first check-up to implants and clear aligners, our dentists explain every option and cost before any treatment starts.

Check-ups

A full examination of teeth, gums and mouth, with X-rays when needed. We recommend a check-up every 6 to 12 months.

Hygiene

Scale and polish, gum health advice and Airflow stain removal with our hygienists. No dentist referral needed.

Fillings & crowns

Tooth-coloured fillings, crowns, bridges and root canal treatment to repair and protect damaged teeth.

Whitening

Dentist-supervised home whitening with custom trays. A safe, gradual way to lift shades over two weeks.

Clear aligners

Nearly invisible braces for adults and teens. Free 20-minute consultation with a 3D scan of your teeth.

Implants

Permanent replacement for missing teeth. We assess bone, health and budget at a consultation first.

Children's dentistry

Gentle first visits, fluoride varnish and fissure sealants. Children can usually be seen on the same visit as a parent.

Nervous patients

Longer appointments, a "stop" signal, numbing gel before injections and IV sedation on selected Thursdays.

Emergency care

Same-day slots kept every weekday and on Saturday morning for pain, swelling, broken teeth and lost fillings.

NHS and private

We see both NHS and private patients

NHS dentistry

We hold an NHS contract and offer NHS check-ups and treatment.

  • Children (under 18) are accepted as new NHS patients now, with no waiting list. NHS treatment is free for under 18s.
  • Adults can join our NHS waiting list. The current wait for a new adult NHS appointment is around 8 weeks.
  • NHS treatment is free if you are pregnant, have had a baby in the last 12 months, are under 19 and in full-time education, or receive certain benefits. Please bring proof.
  • NHS charges are set nationally: Band 1 (check-up) £27.40, Band 2 (fillings, extractions, root canal) £75.30, Band 3 (crowns, dentures, bridges) £326.70. Urgent NHS care is charged at Band 1.

Private dentistry

Private patients get longer appointments, evening and Saturday slots, and treatments not available on the NHS such as whitening, clear aligners and implants.

  • New private patients are accepted now and can usually be seen within a week.
  • NHS patients can choose private treatment for some items, such as white fillings on back teeth. Your dentist will explain the difference.
  • See our private fees and the Unreal Care Plan.

Dental emergency?

We keep emergency appointments free every day we are open. Call 01632 960 000 from 8:00am on weekdays or 9:00am on Saturday, or use the chat, and we will try to see you the same day. You do not need to be registered with us.

Call us for

  • Toothache or pain that will not settle
  • A broken or chipped tooth
  • A lost filling or crown
  • A knocked-out adult tooth (call immediately, keep the tooth in milk)
  • Swelling in the gum or face that is not affecting breathing

When we are closed

Evenings, Sundays and bank holidays: call NHS 111 or visit 111.nhs.uk. They can book you into an urgent dental service.

Go to A&E or call 999 if

  • Swelling affects your breathing, swallowing or eye
  • Bleeding will not stop after 20 minutes of firm pressure
  • You have a serious injury to your face or jaw

Emergency fees: £95 private, or the NHS urgent care charge of £27.40 for NHS patients (free if you are exempt).

Appointments

Booking, rescheduling and cancellations

Rescheduling and cancelling

  • Please give at least 48 hours' notice to move or cancel an appointment. There is no charge if you do.
  • Private appointments cancelled or moved with less than 48 hours' notice, or missed, are charged a £30 fee (£60 for appointments of 60 minutes or longer).
  • There is no fee for NHS appointments, but after two missed NHS appointments in 12 months we may not be able to offer further routine NHS care.
  • Private appointments of 60 minutes or longer need a £50 deposit when booking, which comes off your bill.
  • You can reschedule by phone, by chat, or by replying to your reminder text.

Reminders and running late

  • We send a text reminder 2 days before your appointment and an email the week before.
  • If you are more than 10 minutes late we may need to rebook you, as later patients would be kept waiting.
  • New patients should arrive 10 minutes early to complete a medical history form, or fill it in online from the link in your confirmation.
  • Please bring a list of any medicines you take.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are you open on Saturdays?

Yes. We are open every Saturday from 9:00am to 1:00pm for check-ups, hygiene, children's appointments and emergencies. We are closed on Sundays and bank holidays.

Do you take NHS patients?

Yes. We accept new NHS patients under 18 now. Adults can join our NHS waiting list, currently around 8 weeks. Private appointments are available within about a week.

Do you see children?

Yes, from their first tooth. NHS care for under 18s is free, and children can be booked alongside a parent.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking for patients behind the practice (12 spaces, 2 accessible bays), entered from Make-Believe Road.

Is there a fee to reschedule?

No, if you give at least 48 hours' notice. Private appointments moved, cancelled or missed with less notice cost £30 (£60 for 60+ minute appointments). There is no fee for NHS appointments.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call 01632 960 000 or use the chat when we are open and we will try to see you the same day. Out of hours, call NHS 111. Go to A&E or call 999 if swelling affects breathing, swallowing or your eye, or bleeding will not stop.

Do I need to register with you to get an emergency appointment?

No. We see non-registered patients in emergencies. The fee is £95 private, or £27.40 on the NHS if you are eligible.

How do I pay?

By card, contactless, Apple Pay or Google Pay at each appointment. Interest-free finance over 12 months is available for private plans over £500.

Can I see a hygienist without a check-up?

Yes. Private hygiene appointments can be booked directly for £75 (30 minutes).

Do you offer sedation for nervous patients?

Yes. Dr Anya Premolar runs an IV sedation clinic on selected Thursdays. Ask for a free nervous patient chat first.
