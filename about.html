The gap we fill

Enterprise buyers get expert help. Mid-market buyers mostly don't.

Large enterprises can lean on dedicated vendor solution teams to shape their contact centre AI strategy. Mid-market organisations are usually left to evaluate complex CCaaS and voice AI platforms alone, against sales teams whose job is to sell their own product.

TalkCX sells vendor-neutral expertise into that gap: one team that understands the CCaaS platform and the conversational AI layer well enough to get the deployment right.

25+ years in the room where contact centre technology gets sold and built, as the solution consultant tasked with proving it works. TalkCX puts that experience to work for mid-market contact centres.
