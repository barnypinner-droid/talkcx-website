About TalkCX

Independent advice, built from inside the industry.

TalkCX exists because mid-market contact centres deserve the same calibre of CCaaS and conversational AI guidance that enterprise buyers get from vendor solution teams — without the vendor bias.

The Gap We Fill

Enterprise buyers get expert help. Mid-market buyers mostly don't.

Large enterprises can lean on dedicated vendor solution teams to shape their contact-centre AI strategy. Mid-market organisations are usually left to evaluate complex CCaaS and voice AI platforms alone, against sales teams whose job is to sell their own product.

TalkCX sells vendor-neutral expertise into that gap: a single desk that understands the CCaaS platform, the conversational AI layer, and the workforce management impact of getting it right.

Our Values

  • ◆  Vendor-neutral — we recommend what's right for you, not what pays us the most.
  • ◆  Transparent — every partner relationship and referral fee is disclosed upfront.
  • ◆  Outcome-focused — success is measured in containment, CSAT and agent time saved, not licences sold.
Background

Working in contact centre, telecoms and AI for 25+ years

Founder Barny Pinner has spent that time in the room where contact centre technology actually gets sold and built — not as an outside observer, but as the solution consultant tasked with proving it works.

TCS — TeleCenter System

Started at the company behind TeleCenter System, the first workforce management product on the market — years before "workforce management" was even a recognised category.

NewVoiceMedia

Sales engineering for NewVoiceMedia, the first company to deliver ACD genuinely in the cloud, in a category the industry would only later settle on calling CCaaS. Since absorbed into Vonage.

DigitalGenius — early AI case-routing

Early at DigitalGenius, one of the first companies anywhere to use AI and machine learning to read and respond to inbound digital messages rather than simply route them — building case-routing capability years before anyone called it conversational AI.

Talkdesk, Netcall, Aspect Communications & NICE CXone

Built the pre-sales function at each, breaking ground as the first Sales Engineer into the UK market for more than one of them along the way.

Solution Consultant — PolyAI

Since April 2025, Solution Consultant at PolyAI, one of the leading conversational voice AI companies — the team's top performer by deals closed, building the demos, leading the RFP responses, and working the deals that put this technology into production at scale.

TalkCX founded

TalkCX was founded to put 25+ years of enterprise contact centre, telecoms and AI experience to work for mid-market contact centres, as an independent consultancy and official PolyAI reseller partner.

Track Record

Inside the RFPs that decide how Britain answers the phone

A client list spanning banking, travel, retail, telecoms and the public sector — including British Airways, EasyJet, Royal London, Samsung, Barclays, RBS, HMRC, KLM, Vodafone and Deutsche Telekom.
