Out of Hours AI call answering

Never send an evening or weekend caller to voicemail again. When you're closed, an AI receptionist answers, finds out what the caller needs, takes their details and sends you a summary, ready for the morning.

What it does

  • Answers every call when you're closed: evenings, weekends, bank holidays and lunchtimes
  • Finds out what the caller needs and takes their name and number
  • Texts or emails the caller to confirm you'll be in touch
  • Sends you a summary of every call
  • Answers website chat, WhatsApp or SMS messages out of hours
  • Uses your opening times and your own greeting

It doesn't answer during opening hours or book appointments. For that, see In-House or Connected.

Out of Hours: common questions

Is there a contract?

Yes, 24 months per site with free setup, like a phone contract. A 12-month contract is available with a £295 setup fee.

What if I use more than my allowance?

It keeps working. Extra use is 10p a token (20p per voice minute, 10p per digital conversation), billed monthly. If it happens often, we'll suggest a bigger package.

Can I change package later?

Yes. You can move up a package at any time.

Will callers know it's an AI?

Yes. The agent says it's an AI assistant at the start of the call, and callers can ask for a person.

Which AI is it?

It runs on PolyAI. TalkCX is an official PolyAI reseller partner and sets it up and supports it for you.

Do prices include VAT?

No. All prices exclude VAT.
