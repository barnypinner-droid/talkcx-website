Data Processing Agreement.

How TalkCX processes personal data on behalf of its customers under UK data protection law.

Version 1.0 · Effective 4 November 2026 · Last updated November 2026

This Data Processing Agreement (the DPA) forms part of the agreement between TalkCX Ltd, company number 17529587, registered office 66 Paul Street, London, EC2A 4NA, United Kingdom (TalkCX, we, us), and the customer named in an Order Form (Customer, you), made under the TalkCX Master Services Agreement or any other written agreement for TalkCX services that refers to this DPA (the Agreement). It applies whenever we process Customer Personal Data on your behalf. Capitalised words not defined here have the meaning given in the Agreement.

1. Definitions

Data Protection Laws
the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003, and, where they apply to the processing, the EU GDPR and national laws implementing it, in each case as amended or replaced.
UK GDPR
the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 as it forms part of UK law.
Customer Personal Data
Personal Data within Customer Data that we process on your behalf in providing the Services, as described in Annex 1.
Sub-processor
any third party, including the Platform Provider, that we engage to process Customer Personal Data.
Restricted Transfer
a transfer of Customer Personal Data from the UK to a country not covered by UK adequacy regulations, or, where the EU GDPR applies, from the EEA to a country without an EU adequacy decision.
UK Transfer Mechanism
the International Data Transfer Agreement or the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, issued by the Information Commissioner under section 119A of the Data Protection Act 2018.

"Controller", "processor", "data subject", "personal data", "personal data breach", "processing" and "special category data" have the meanings given in the UK GDPR.

2. Roles and scope

2.1 Controller and processor. You are the controller of Customer Personal Data and we are your processor. The Platform Provider and our other Sub-processors process Customer Personal Data as our sub-processors.

2.2 Our own data. We are a controller of the business contact details of your staff that we use to manage our relationship with you (for example to send invoices, support updates and service notices), and we process that data under our privacy policy. This DPA does not apply to that data.

2.3 Platform Provider purposes. If the Vendor Terms allow the Platform Provider to use Customer Personal Data for its own purposes (for example to improve its speech or language models), the Order Form will say so and describe any opt-out available to you. Where an opt-out is available and you ask us to, we will exercise it on your behalf.

2.4 Precedence. If this DPA conflicts with the Agreement on any matter relating to Customer Personal Data, this DPA prevails.

3. Your obligations as controller

3.1 You are responsible for: (a) having a lawful basis for the processing described in Annex 1, and an Article 9 condition for any special category data; (b) giving End Users clear privacy information, including that they may be speaking to an AI agent and that calls may be recorded and transcribed; (c) obtaining any consents required by Data Protection Laws; and (d) the accuracy of Customer Personal Data you provide.

3.2 Your instructions to us must comply with Data Protection Laws. You will only provide the Customer Personal Data needed for the Services.

3.3 You will not use the Services to collect payment card details unless a PCI DSS compliant configuration has been agreed in the Order Form.

4. Our obligations as processor

4.1 Instructions. We will process Customer Personal Data only on your documented instructions, unless UK law requires otherwise (in which case we will tell you before processing, unless the law prohibits it). Your instructions are the Agreement, this DPA, the configuration of your AI agents, and any other written instructions you give us that are consistent with the Agreement. We will tell you promptly if we believe an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws.

4.2 Confidentiality. We will ensure that anyone authorised to process Customer Personal Data is bound by a duty of confidentiality and only accesses it as needed to provide the Services.

4.3 Security. We will implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Customer Personal Data, taking into account the state of the art, cost, the nature of the processing and the risks to individuals. These include the measures in Annex 2. We may update these measures provided the overall level of protection is not reduced.

4.4 Assistance. Taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to us, we will give you reasonable assistance with your obligations relating to security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation with the Information Commissioner. We may charge reasonable fees for assistance beyond what is needed to provide the Services, except where the need arises from our breach.

4.5 No sale or unrelated use. We will not sell Customer Personal Data, use it for our own marketing, or combine it with other data except as needed to provide the Services.

4.6 Records. We will keep the records of processing required of processors by Article 30(2) of the UK GDPR.

5. Special category data and sensitive data

5.1 Some customers, such as dental and healthcare practices, may use the Services in a way that involves End Users sharing health information, for example when booking an appointment. Where you expect this, you will tell us before Go-Live and it will be recorded in the Order Form or SOW, so that suitable configuration can be agreed, such as limiting what the AI agent asks, redacting transcripts and setting shorter retention periods.

5.2 AI agents should be designed to collect only the minimum special category data needed for the purpose, and not to collect it at all where it is not needed.

5.3 The Services are not designed for, or directed at, children. You will configure AI agents appropriately where children may contact you, for example by routing them to a human.

6. Sub-processors

6.1 Authorisation. You give us general authorisation to engage Sub-processors. The Sub-processors in Annex 3 are approved as at the date you sign the Order Form.

6.2 Our commitments. We will carry out reasonable due diligence on each Sub-processor, put in place a written contract that imposes data protection obligations on it that are materially no less protective than this DPA, and remain liable to you for its performance of those obligations.

6.3 Changes. We will tell you by email at least 30 days before engaging a new Sub-processor or replacing an existing one, and update Annex 3. Where the Platform Provider gives us less notice of a change to its own sub-processors, we will pass the notice on to you within 5 Business Days of receiving it.

6.4 Objections. You may object to a new Sub-processor on reasonable data protection grounds by writing to us within 15 days of our notice. We will discuss your concerns in good faith and try to offer a reasonable alternative. If we cannot resolve the objection within 30 days, you may terminate the affected Services by written notice and we will refund any prepaid Fees for the period after termination.

7. Data subject requests

7.1 If we receive a request from a data subject relating to Customer Personal Data, we will forward it to you within 5 Business Days and will not respond to it ourselves, except to confirm it has been passed to you.

7.2 We will give you reasonable assistance, including through the features of the Platform, to respond to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights, such as finding, exporting or deleting call recordings and transcripts.

8. Personal data breaches

8.1 Notification. We will notify you without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data. This gives you time to meet your own 72-hour notification duty to the Information Commissioner where it applies.

8.2 Information. Our notice will describe, as far as we know at the time, the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate numbers of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, the measures taken or proposed, and a contact for further information. We will provide further information as it becomes available.

8.3 Response. We will take reasonable steps to contain and investigate the breach, work with the relevant Sub-processor, and cooperate with you in meeting your obligations. We will not notify regulators or data subjects about a breach of Customer Personal Data on your behalf unless you ask us to or the law requires it.

8.4 Our notification of a breach is not an admission of fault or liability.

9. Audits and information

9.1 On request, we will make available the information reasonably needed to demonstrate our compliance with this DPA, including summaries of our security measures and any third-party certifications or audit reports held by the Platform Provider (such as ISO 27001 certificates or SOC 2 reports), subject to confidentiality.

9.2 If that information is not reasonably sufficient, or where a regulator requires it or following a personal data breach, you may audit our compliance with this DPA once in any 12-month period, on at least 30 days' written notice, during business hours, and at your own cost. You or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality may carry out the audit, and you will take reasonable steps to minimise disruption to our business.

9.3 Audits of the Platform Provider or other Sub-processors are limited to the audit rights and reports those Sub-processors make available to us, which we will exercise on your behalf where reasonably required.

10. International transfers

10.1 We aim to host and process Customer Personal Data in the UK or the EEA where the Platform allows it. The hosting region for each Order Form will be stated in it.

10.2 We will not make, or allow a Sub-processor to make, a Restricted Transfer unless it is covered by UK adequacy regulations, an EU adequacy decision (where relevant), a UK Transfer Mechanism, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or another lawful transfer mechanism, together with any supplementary measures the law requires.

10.3 You authorise the transfers listed in Annex 3, on the basis of the safeguards described there.

11. Return and deletion

11.1 During the term. Call recordings and transcripts are kept for the retention period configured for your AI agents, as recorded in the Order Form or SOW. You may ask us to change retention settings at any time.

11.2 On termination. You may ask us within 30 days after the Agreement ends to provide an export of Customer Personal Data in a commonly used format, to the extent the Platform allows. After that period, we will delete Customer Personal Data, and instruct our Sub-processors to do the same, within 90 days, unless UK law requires us to keep it.

11.3 Backups. Customer Personal Data in backups will be deleted as the backups expire under normal rotation, and will remain protected under this DPA until then.

11.4 On request, we will confirm in writing that deletion has been completed.

12. Liability and general terms

12.1 Each party's liability under or in connection with this DPA is subject to the limitations and exclusions of liability in the Agreement. Nothing in this DPA limits either party's liability to data subjects under Data Protection Laws.

12.2 This DPA lasts for as long as we process Customer Personal Data, including after the Agreement ends.

12.3 If a change in Data Protection Laws, or guidance from the Information Commissioner, requires changes to this DPA, the parties will agree those changes in good faith. We may update this DPA from time to time, but no update will reduce the protection given to Customer Personal Data during the term of an Order Form without your written agreement.

12.4 This DPA is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.

Annex 1: Details of processing

Subject matter
Provision of the Services under the Agreement, including AI voice and chat agents for your customers, callers and patients.
Duration
The term of the Agreement and the deletion period in clause 11.
Nature of processing
Receiving and handling calls and messages; speech recognition and transcription; natural language understanding and response generation; call recording; storage; transfer of calls and information to your staff; integration with your booking, practice management, CRM and telephony systems; analytics and reporting; testing, quality assurance and troubleshooting; support.
Purpose
To answer and route End User enquiries, take and manage bookings and other requests on your behalf, and provide you with reporting, support and improvements to your AI agents.
Data subjects
Your callers, customers, patients and prospective customers or patients (End Users); people calling on their behalf, such as parents, carers or representatives; your staff and contractors who use the Platform or are named in AI agent content.
Categories of personal data
Name; phone number and caller ID; email and postal address; date of birth or other identity verification details; voice recordings; call and chat transcripts; appointment, booking, order or account details; reference numbers; the content of enquiries; call metadata such as time, duration and outcome; staff names and user account details.
Special category data
Only where recorded in the Order Form or SOW under clause 5: health information that End Users share, such as the reason for a dental or medical appointment, symptoms, or accessibility needs. The voice recordings themselves are not processed for the purpose of uniquely identifying anyone, so are not treated as biometric data, unless the Order Form specifies voice authentication.
Frequency
Continuous, for as long as AI agents are live.
Retention
As configured and recorded in the Order Form or SOW, then as set out in clause 11.

Annex 2: Security measures

We, and the Platform Provider for the Platform, maintain measures including:

  • Encryption: Customer Personal Data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and encrypted at rest.
  • Access control: access on a least-privilege, need-to-know basis; multi-factor authentication for administrative access; prompt removal of access when no longer needed; access logging.
  • People: confidentiality obligations for all staff and contractors; data protection and security training.
  • Data minimisation: AI agents designed to collect only what is needed; transcript redaction and configurable retention where the Platform supports them; production Customer Personal Data not used for testing unless needed and agreed.
  • Platform security: hosting with reputable cloud providers holding recognised certifications such as ISO 27001; network security controls; vulnerability management and regular patching; penetration testing by the Platform Provider.
  • Resilience: backups, redundancy and business continuity arrangements designed to restore availability and access to data in a timely manner.
  • Incident response: documented procedures to detect, contain, investigate and report security incidents.
  • Supplier management: due diligence and written data protection terms with all Sub-processors.
  • Our own systems: project materials containing Customer Personal Data (such as test call samples) are kept in access-controlled business accounts with multi-factor authentication, and deleted when no longer needed.
