Where to start

Contact Centre AI readiness assessment

Before you spend money on AI, find out where it will actually help. We look at your calls, your systems and your team, and tell you plainly which use cases are worth automating, which aren't, and what it would cost.

What's included

  • Review of your current call types, volumes and peaks
  • Look at your phone system, CRM and booking or case management tools
  • Interviews with team leaders and front-line agents
  • Shortlist of the use cases best suited to a voice or digital AI agent
  • Business case with costs, expected savings and risks
  • Recommended next step: pilot, RFP or not yet

AI Readiness Assessment: common questions

Do we need to have chosen a vendor first?

No. The assessment is vendor-neutral and often happens before you've spoken to anyone.

What if AI isn't right for us?

Then the report says so. Sometimes the better answer is a process or IVR change, and we'll tell you that.

What do you need from us?

Access to call reports or recordings, an hour or two with a few team members, and someone who knows your systems.
